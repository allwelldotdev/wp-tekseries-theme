About this event

A case study in perseverance to progressive impact.

This is an interactive learning program, designed as an introduction and supplement to current school level computer skills, helping children build a foundation for today’s job market.

The camp is comprised of 8-16 year olds, some of whom had never turned on a computer when they first started the after school program. 12-18 months from finding the power button on a laptop, most of the kids now code and design games using Scratch & PictoBlox AI programming, build an entire Robot Car and design Event Cards and Flyers.

This reaffirms what we all know, that talent knows no boundaries and is the reason, we remain even more committed to improving and expanding our program.
