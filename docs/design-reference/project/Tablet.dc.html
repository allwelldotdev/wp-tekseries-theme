    The Challenge

    Nigeria faces severe youth employment challenges, with youth unemployment reaching 42.5% and underemployment at 21.5%. Over 28 million jobs in Nigeria are projected to require digital skills by 2030, yet a structural gap exists between acquiring basic certificates and securing gainful employment or seed support.

  • conversion_path

    Bridge to Employment

    A free, curated portal, JobSeries highlights City and regional job opportunities, to match job-ready youth with employers seeking interns, entry-level, and mid-level tech talent. Applicants can upload their CVs and Resumes, perform free job searches and attend periodic interviews, assessments and CV workshops.

  • handshake

    Employer Partner Network

    Partnering with corporate entities and startups to create internship pathways that serve as gateways to long-term tech careers.
