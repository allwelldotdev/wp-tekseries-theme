About this event

Ladies, ready to build your future in engineering?

Engineering needs more women, and we’re here to help you take your place! Whether you’re a student wondering if engineering is the right path, a fresh graduate figuring out your next step, or a young engineer ready to grow, this session is for you.

Join us for the “Women in Engineering Series,” where we get real about the skills you need, the challenges women face in the field, and the opportunities waiting for you. You’ll hear straight from recent engineering graduates who’ve been in your shoes, and from a talent acquisition leader who knows exactly what recruiters look for.

This isn’t just a talk. It’s your chance to learn, ask questions, and connect with people who can help you move forward. Stay for our Recruiter Exposition & Q&A, and bring your toughest questions!
